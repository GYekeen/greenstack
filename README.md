GreenStack — Tokenized Carbon Credit Protocol
GreenStack tokenizes carbon credits on the Stacks blockchain, enabling transparent climate impact tracking,
decentralized trading, and proof-of-retirement for ESG compliance.
A carbon credit = 1 ton of CO₂ reduced/removed, verified by environmental auditors.
This contract ensures no double spending of carbon offset value.

Use Cases
Organizations offsetting operational emissions
Carbon credit secondary marketplace
Proof-of-green compliance for Web3 & corporate sustainability
Community and NGO climate projects

Key Features
Carbon credit issuance tied to verified projects
Transferable as a digital asset
Public, immutable retirement tracking
Supports auditors and project origin metadata
Prevents reactivation after retirement
Clear event logs for carbon footprint reporting
